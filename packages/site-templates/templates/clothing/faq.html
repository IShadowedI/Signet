Ordering

Browse our collections and add items to your bag. When you're ready, click the shopping bag icon to view your cart, then click "Checkout" to complete your purchase. You can check out as a guest or create an account for faster checkout next time.
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placing them. Please contact us as soon as possible with your order number and we'll do our best to help. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled.
Yes! We offer complimentary gift wrapping on all orders. Simply select the "Gift Wrap" option at checkout. You can also add a personalised message card.
Enter your discount code in the "Discount Code" field at checkout before completing your payment. Discount codes are case-insensitive and cannot be combined with other promotions. Only one code can be applied per order.

Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping is free on all orders over $59. For orders under $59, standard shipping costs $4.99. Express shipping (1–2 business days) is available for $9.99 regardless of order value.
Standard delivery: 3–5 business days. Express delivery: 1–2 business days. International orders: 7–14 business days. These are estimates and may vary during busy periods.
Yes, we ship to over 80 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and estimated delivery times are shown at checkout. Please note that duties, taxes, and customs fees may apply and are the responsibility of the recipient.
Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link. You can also track your order by logging into your account and visiting the "My Orders" section.

Returns & Exchanges

We offer free returns within 30 days of receiving your order. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with tags attached. Sale items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for returns.
Log in to your account, navigate to "My Orders", select the relevant order, and click "Request Return". You'll receive a prepaid returns label by email within 24 hours. Pack your item securely and drop it off at any authorised courier location.
Once we receive your return, we'll inspect the item and process your refund within 3–5 business days. The refund will be applied to your original payment method. Please allow an additional 3–5 business days for the funds to appear in your account.
Yes! To exchange an item, start a return request and select "Exchange" instead of "Refund". Choose your preferred size or colour and we'll send the replacement once we receive your original item. Subject to availability.

Sizing & Fit

Each product page includes a detailed size guide. We recommend measuring yourself (bust, waist, hips) and comparing your measurements to the size chart. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for a more relaxed fit.
Most of our styles run true to size. Where a style has a particularly slim or relaxed cut, we note this on the product page (e.g. "Model recommends sizing up"). Customer reviews also often include helpful fit feedback.
We stock sizes XS–4XL (UK 6–28) across most styles. Our inclusive sizing range is available on selected products, clearly marked with the "Extended Sizes" badge. We're continuously expanding our size range.

Account & Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are encrypted and processed securely via our payment provider.
