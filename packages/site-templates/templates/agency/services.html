How it works

Our engagement process

A clear, repeatable framework that keeps projects on track and clients in the loop from day one.

STEP 01

Discovery Call

A free 30-minute conversation to understand your goals, timeline, and budget. We'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.

STEP 02

Proposal & Kick-off

We send a detailed proposal with scope, timelines, and investment. Once signed, we kick off with a deep-dive workshop.

STEP 03

Iterative Design

Work is shared early and often. You get a Figma link, not a PDF. Feedback rounds are built into the process — no surprises.

STEP 04

Launch & Handover

A smooth go-live with full documentation, training, and a 30-day support window. We don't disappear after delivery.
