Our story

Built different, on purpose.

NEXUS was founded in 2017 by Alex Chen and Sarah Kim — two people who were tired of agencies that prioritized billings over outcomes. We started with four people and a very clear north star: make things that actually work.

Eight years later we've grown to a team of 28 across three offices, but the philosophy is unchanged. We take on fewer clients than most agencies our size because we believe in depth over breadth. Every project gets our full attention, from discovery to delivery.

We've won 42 design awards, but the number we care most about is 98 — that's the percentage of our clients who come back for a second project.

What drives us

Our values

These aren't framed posters on a wall. They're the principles that determine how we hire, how we work, and what we'll turn down.

Craft takes time.

We never rush a project just to hit a billing target. Good work takes the time it takes — we build that into every proposal.

Clients are partners.

We don't do the mysterious black-box agency thing. You'll see work early and often, and your voice shapes the output at every stage.

Outcomes over outputs.

A beautiful website that doesn't convert is a failure. We tie everything back to measurable business results, not just deliverables.

Say no to say yes.

We deliberately limit our client roster. It means turning away revenue, but it also means every client gets our best thinking — not our second best.

Global perspective.

Our team spans three continents and nine nationalities. That diversity of lived experience makes our work more interesting — and more effective globally.

Always learning.

Every team member gets an annual learning budget and protected time to explore new tools, techniques, and ideas outside of client work.

Life at NEXUS

A place where great work feels like the natural default.

We believe the environment you work in shapes the work you create. Our New York studio is designed to encourage serendipitous collaboration — open enough to spark conversation, quiet enough to think deeply.

Every Friday afternoon is maker time — no client work, no meetings. People build side projects, experiment with new tools, or write for the journal.

We offer full remote flexibility, 25 days paid leave, annual learning budget, health coverage, and a twice-yearly team offsite. But more than the perks — we think the work itself is the best benefit.
