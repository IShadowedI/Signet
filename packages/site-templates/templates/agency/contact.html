Common questions

Before you reach out

Brand identity projects typically run 6–10 weeks. A full website project is usually 10–16 weeks. Larger engagements with multiple workstreams can take 4–6 months. We'll always give you a realistic timeline estimate in the proposal — and we stick to it.

Our minimum engagement is $8,000 for a standalone brand project and $15,000 for a web project. We don't take on smaller one-off tasks as standalone projects — but we do offer retainer arrangements for ongoing support from $3,500/month.

Both. About 40% of our clients are startups raising their first brand or building their launch site. 60% are established companies looking to evolve or rebrand. What matters is that you have a real vision for what you're building and the seriousness to see it through.

Very involved, if you want to be. We share work-in-progress regularly via Figma or Notion, hold a weekly check-in, and always ask for client input at strategic decision points. Some clients prefer to stay close throughout; others prefer to review at key milestones. We adapt to you.

Every project includes a defined number of revision rounds — usually 3–5 depending on scope. If we're ever completely misaligned before the halfway point, we offer a structured reset at no extra cost. We've been doing this long enough to know that honest, early feedback saves everyone time.
